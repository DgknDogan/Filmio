App Privacy ready to paste

App Store Connect → App Privacy → Get Started

These answers must match ios/Runner/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy exactly. Apple compares the two, and a mismatch is a rejection that costs a whole review cycle.

The two gate questions

QuestionAnswerWhy
Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app? Yes Firebase stores the account and the liked titles.
Is any collected data used to track the user? No No ad networks, no analytics, no data brokers. This is why there is no ATT prompt.

The four data types to declare

Everything else in the list stays unticked. For each of these: linked to the user — yes, used for tracking — no.

CategoryData typePurpose(s)
Contact Info Email Address App Functionality
Contact Info Name App Functionality
Identifiers User ID App Functionality
User Content Other User Content App Functionality and Product Personalization

Other User Content is the liked films and series. It carries the second purpose because those likes are what the recommendation service reads.

Do not tick: Location, Contacts, Photos, Health, Financial Info, Browsing History, Search History, Diagnostics, Usage Data, Purchases, Sensitive Info. The app asks for none of them, and claiming more than you collect is its own problem.
02

Age Rating read before answering

App Store Connect → your app → Age Rating → Edit

The one that matters is user-generated content. Filmio shows TMDB's reviews, written by strangers, so the honest answer is yes — and the moderation you built is what makes that answer safe rather than damaging.

QuestionAnswerReasoning
User-generated content Yes TMDB reviews. Follow-ups will ask about moderation — you have filtering, reporting, blocking and a published contact address.
Profanity or crude humour Infrequent / Mild The word filter folds flagged reviews, but a word list is never complete.
Horror or fear themes Infrequent / Mild Horror posters and synopses appear in the catalogue.
Violence — realistic or cartoon Infrequent / Mild Film artwork and plot summaries describe it; the app shows no footage of its own.
Alcohol, tobacco or drug references Infrequent / Mild Same reason — it turns up in synopses.
Sexual content or nudity None include_adult=false is sent on every search and discover call.
Unrestricted web access No No in-app browser. A trailer is handed to the YouTube app or Safari, which is not web access inside the app.
Gambling, contests, medical info No None present.
Expect 12+ or higher. Do not try to talk it down to 4+ — a catalogue app carrying unmoderated third-party reviews cannot honestly claim 4+, and being caught understating it is worse than the rating itself. Apple revised this questionnaire recently, so the wording you see may differ from the labels above; answer by meaning.

Screenshots you capture

Simulator → iPhone 17 Pro Max → ⌘S saves to the desktop

The 6.9-inch set is the one that is required — 1320 × 2868 portrait. App Store Connect scales that down for smaller devices, so one set is enough unless you want to art-direct the others. Up to ten; three good ones beat ten dull ones.

The five to take, in this order

  1. Films tab, scrolled to the top — the recommended title filling the head of the page. This is the shot that says what the app is.
  2. A details screen — artwork, score, genres, synopsis. Pick a title with good artwork; it is doing half the work.
  3. The filter sheet, open — genre chips selected, a year range set. This is the feature that separates you from every other TMDB client.
  4. A browse result — the filtered grid with the result count visible.
  5. Account tab, signed in — the two lists with real likes in them.
Never the sign-in screen. Guideline 2.3.3 treats a login screen as a screenshot of nothing. It is also the least interesting thing you built.

Notes for App Review fill in two lines

Prepare for Submission → App Review Information → Notes

This is the highest-value box on the whole form. Every guideline finding you fixed is answered here before the reviewer has to go looking for it — and the two things they will definitely try, guest mode and account deletion, are the first two things they read.

Fill in the demo account yourself. I have left the two credential lines blank on purpose — passwords are not something to hand around. Use a real account you are happy to have deleted, because they will delete it.
Review notes

The app can be used without signing in. On the sign-in screen, tap
"Look around without an account". The full catalogue is available in this
mode: films, series, search, filters, details, trailers and reviews.

DEMO ACCOUNT
Account-based features — liking titles, the two personal lists,

  E-mail:   [FILL IN]
  Password: [FILL IN]

ACCOUNT DELETION (guideline 5.1.1(v))
Account tab -> gear icon (top right) -> "ACCOUNT REMOVAL" -> "Delete account".
The password is asked for again to confirm. Deletion removes the account and
the user's stored data — liked films and series — permanently.

PRIVACY POLICY (guideline 5.1.1(i))
In the app: Account tab -> gear icon -> "ABOUT" -> "Privacy Policy".
On the web: https://dgkndogan.github.io/Filmio/privacy-policy.html

USER-GENERATED CONTENT (guideline 1.2)
Reviews are supplied by the TMDB API and are written by TMDB's users. Each
review card has a "..." menu offering:
  - Report review — five reasons; a report is filed to a moderation queue that
    is read by a person and answered within three days, and the review is
    hidden on that device immediately
  - Hide reviews by this author — blocks that author on the device
A word filter also folds any review containing offensive language behind a
warning, which the reader can open deliberately.
Contact address for reports and support: support.filmio@gmail.com — also shown
in the app under gear icon -> "ABOUT" -> "Contact support".

TRAILERS (guideline 5.2.3)
Filmio embeds no video player and re-streams nothing. Tapping a trailer hands
the YouTube watch link to the system, which opens the YouTube app if it is
installed and Safari if it is not. Nothing is downloaded, extracted or cached.

THIRD-PARTY DATA (guideline 5.2.2)
Catalogue data comes from the TMDB API. The attribution TMDB's terms require is
shown in the app under gear icon -> "ABOUT".

WHAT THE APP DOES NOT DO
No in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no advertising, no analytics SDK, no
tracking and no App Tracking Transparency prompt. No permission is requested
for location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

The app is iPhone only.

Before You Hit Submit

The last pass, in ten minutes

Already true verified

  • Bundle ID com.dogukan.filmio across iOS, Android and Firebase
  • Privacy manifest ships inside Runner.app; every third-party SDK carries its own
  • ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption set to false — no export compliance prompt
  • iPhone only; no iPad screenshots needed
  • App icon in all fifteen sizes, no alpha channel
  • Privacy policy live at the URL above
  • 380 tests green, analyzer clean, release build succeeds

Check by hand five minutes

  • Build the release with the token. --dart-define-from-file=env.json must be in the archive command. Without TMDB_TOKEN the app throws on launch, and that is an instant 2.1 rejection.
  • Report a review once and confirm the document lands in the ReviewReport collection. If the Firestore rule is wrong you get permission-denied and the reviewer will find it.
  • Open the trailer on a real device and confirm YouTube takes over.
  • Read the App Privacy answers back against the four rows in step 01.
  • Watch the support inbox from the day you submit. Review does write to it sometimes.
